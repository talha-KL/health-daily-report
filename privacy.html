Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Who uses this app

Health Daily Report is a personal automation used only by its owner. It is not distributed to other people.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app requests read-only access to Google Health data: activity and fitness, health metrics and measurements, sleep, and nutrition. It cannot change or delete any health data.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is stored only in a Google Sheet in the owner's own Google account.
  • A daily summary is emailed to the owner's own email address.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • OAuth credentials are stored in the script's private settings and are never included in reports or logs.

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete the spreadsheet and revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
